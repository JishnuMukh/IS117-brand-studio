## Ethical persuasion

Choose two applications that help the visitor make a voluntary, informed decision. Record their real implementation and mechanism rather than a principle name alone. Required size selection or validation alone does not establish consistency. Do not invent social proof, scarcity, authority, or product facts.

| Principle   | Actual copy or interaction                                                                                                                                | Visitor benefit / factual support                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Unity       | “For the days you already have” section features hot-Sunday errands first, followed by lounging, long flights, and relaxed brunch.                        | Learner expects the specific public-wear situation to make the comfort need recognizable and connect to the zero-thought promise. Visible bullets say the scenario makes intended use concrete and supports comparison with a visitor’s own routine. This is a hypothesis from the audience brief, not customer research; heat, sweat, breathability, fit, and appearance claims are unverified. |
| Reciprocity | Optional “Free tee checklist” disclosure leads with fabric composition and weight, then offers measurements, care, and construction as comparison points. | Learner expects fabric information to help comfort-focused visitors judge whether a tee may suit their preferences over extended wear and washing. Visible bullets explain how the checklist may help visitors compare details, consider their preferences, and notice missing specifications. This is a hypothesis; no softness, irritation, or shape-retention results are verified.           |
