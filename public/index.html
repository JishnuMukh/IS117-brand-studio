Free tee checklist: what to compare

Before choosing any tee, compare details that matter to you:

  • Garment measurements against a shirt you already like
  • Fabric composition and fabric weight
  • Care instructions
  • Collar, seams, and hem construction

This is general guidance, not a spec sheet for Staple Cut. Verified measurements, materials, care instructions, and construction details are not available for this concept.
